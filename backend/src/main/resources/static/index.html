About DIET Attendance AI

DIET Attendance AI is designed to provide a unified academic management experience for students, faculty, Heads of Departments and administrators. The platform organizes academic information department-wise and provides role-specific dashboards for attendance, subjects, timetables, reports and academic monitoring. The system is designed to connect with an authorized online institute directory so that institutional information can be managed without requiring a local database in the application.
